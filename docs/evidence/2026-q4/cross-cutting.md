# Cross-Cutting Sources: 2026 Q4 Evidence Notes

Run `2026-q4`. None of these sources is scored under the v2 method; they inform the [v3 shadow](../../methodology/v3-shadow.md).

## Reach Anchors

- **U.S. Census Bureau Business Trends and Outlook Survey (BTOS).** 23.8% of U.S. firms reported using AI in the collection period ending 2026-10-04. Sector rates: finance and insurance 40.1%, information 50.2%, professional services 42.7%, health care 24.8%, educational services 39.9%, administrative and support 21.7% (credit intermediation 23.9%, securities 51.2%). The question changed on 2025-11-17 from AI "in producing goods or services" to AI "in any of its business functions"; the national rate jumped from 10.0% to 17.3% at the change and Census publishes no bridge, so any series must start there. BTOS measures how many firms use AI, not how much work is delegated: <https://census.gov/library/stories/2026/05/ai-use-businesses.html>.
- **BTOS 2026 AI supplement** (Nov 2025-Feb 2026): 10.1% of AI-using firms report AI doing a task previously done by an employee; AI in HR 4.2%, customer service 7.6%, legal 7.1%, IT 11.4% of all firms.
- International cross-checks: Eurostat (19.95% of EU firms with 10+ staff using AI in 2025), the UK ONS business survey, Statistics Canada, and the Australian Bureau of Statistics.

## Depth Signals

- **Real-Time Population Survey (via FRED):** 6.27% of work hours assisted by generative AI in Q2 2026, and 45.2% of workers using generative AI for work. Hours share is the closest representative measure of delegated work found.
- **Anthropic Economic Index** (June 2026 release): 48.6% of Claude.ai conversations and 94.2% of API traffic classified as automation rather than augmentation. Reproducible from published data, but one vendor's traffic, so context only.

## Mapping To Domains

Use the finest reliable industry cells for credit (NAICS 522), algorithmic trading (523), and medical (621). For hiring, support, legal, and software development, business-function or occupation series fit better than industry codes. A firm-adoption share adds little for content moderation and algorithmic trading.
