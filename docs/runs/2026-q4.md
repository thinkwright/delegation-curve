## Source Lock

The full ledger is [2026-q4-source-ledger.csv](2026-q4-source-ledger.csv); domain notes are under [docs/evidence/2026-q4](../evidence/2026-q4/).

**Refreshed**

- **Meta Automated Detection** 94.2% (Q2 2026): proactive rate weighted by content actioned across Facebook and Instagram, 13 policy areas each, excluding Fake Accounts and Spam, computed from Meta's official CSER CSV.
- **YouTube Automated Flagging** 98.6% (Apr-Jun 2026): 11,005,420 of 11,159,676 removed videos were first flagged by automated systems.
- **TikTok Automated Enforcement** 94.1% (H1 2026): seventh DSA transparency report.
- **Agentic Task Delegation** 19.8%: the GitHub-trace component reruns the same method on the same 127,670 projects through early April 2026 (arXiv:2606.07448).
- **FDA AI-Enabled Medical Devices** 1,614: direct count of the FDA list (current as of 2026-09-04).
- **Tool Ecosystem Reach** 93.2M: VS Code Marketplace, collected 2026-10-17.
- **AI-Underwritten Personal Loan Proxy**: the Upstart factor is confirmed at 91% in the Q1 and Q2 2026 10-Qs. The value stays 38.2% because TransUnion's Q2 2026 fintech share could not be confirmed from its published text, so the Q3 2025 denominator (42%) is held.

**Corrected** (logged in the [restatement and correction log](../methodology/restatement-policy.md#log))

- **YouTube Automated Flagging**: the 2026 Q2 value (99.5%) was the automated share of *comment* removals for Jul-Sep 2023. The video share for the intended window (Jul-Sep 2025) was 97.9%.
- **Meta Automated Detection**: the 2026 Q2 value (95.2%) could not be reproduced from Meta's data. The documented method used now gives 94.5% for Q3 2025.
- **AI Credit Decisioning (Banks)**: the 2026 Q2 value (20%) was the share of 24 North American credit-risk chiefs with at least one generative-AI use case (October 2023), not deployment in credit decisions. It is replaced by the ECB's share of euro-area significant banks using AI for credit scoring (30% in 2024). The geography differs from the indicator's original U.S. framing.

**Label and window fixes** (no value change): buy-side AI trading survey dated to its Jul-Sep 2024 fieldwork; HireVue platform volume dated to Q1 2024; Thomson Reuters legal adoption dated to its Oct-Nov 2025 fieldwork; Sonar/GitLab output share dated to late 2025.

**Deferred as methodology changes** (recorded as v3 candidates, not used under the frozen method)

- JetBrains Developer Ecosystem 2026 (90% of developers use AI coding agents weekly) differs from the AI Pulse construct behind AI Workflow Reliance.
- U.S. K-12 teacher surveys (NPR/Ipsos 62%, Gallup-Walton 60%) would change the Teachers Using AI population from OECD lower-secondary teachers.
- Fin's AI resolution rate is single-vendor and changed definition in July 2026, so it cannot replace Bot Deflection Rate.
- A legal-only cut of Thomson Reuters adoption (law firms 41%, corporate legal 47%) would narrow the construct.
